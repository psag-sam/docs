---
title: "Contact Details View"
---

Clicking any contact name in the main dashboard table opens the comprehensive 360-degree **Contact Detail View**. This page gives you full context for every interaction with this individual.

## Header Control Bar

At the top of the profile, the Title Banner displays the person's full name alongside quick action icons:

- **Email:** Trigger an email communication directly from the portal.
- **Edit:** Open the profile editor to update their details.
- **Clone:** Duplicate the contact details to quickly create a similar profile (e.g., for a colleague at the same office).
- **Delete:** Move the contact record to the trash/archive.

---

## Left Panel: Profile Information

The left side of the screen breaks down the contact's static data into easy-to-read cards:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Basic & Account Information" icon="id-badge">
    - **Basic Info:** Displays their Name, Email, Phone numbers, Department, and any saved Notes.
    - **Account Info:** Shows their linked parent Account, Primary Contact status badge, Contact Status, and Role.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Address & Custom Fields" icon="location-dot">
    - **Address:** Comprehensive mailing block showing their physical location.
    - **Custom Field Information:** Displays any custom attributes extended for your workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Audit Information" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    System audit trail tracking exactly who created the record and when, as well as the most recent modifications.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---

## Right Panel: Interaction & Collaboration

The right-hand sidebar tracks all dynamic events, history, and internal collaboration regarding this person.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Activities Panel">
    Click **ADD ACTIVITY** to schedule and track tasks, upcoming calls, and follow-up meetings associated directly with this contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attachments Panel">
    Click **ADD ATTACHMENT** to upload and securely store document files, signed contracts, or identification copies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Echo Feed Panel">
    Click **ADD MESSAGE** in this centralized collaboration feed. You can log team notes, post updates, and tag other users regarding this specific contact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

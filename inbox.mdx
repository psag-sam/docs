The **Email Inbox** module in **Pearl CRM** provides an integrated email management interface that syncs directly with external email providers (e.g., Gmail) to streamline customer support and sales communications without leaving the CRM environment.

![Screenshot 2026 09 30 105854](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-30-105854.png "Screenshot 2026 09 30 105854")

## Overview

The Email Inbox aggregates incoming customer inquiries, system notifications, and outbound messages into a dual-pane reader. Communications sent or received through the inbox can be linked to corresponding accounts, leads, deals, or support cases.

## Workspace Navigation & Mail Folders

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Account Profile & Compose" icon="user-gear" color="#3B82F6">
    - **Signed In Account**: Displays the currently linked email account (e.g., `Signed in with Gmail`). Click the gear icon to manage integration settings.
    - **Compose Button**: Opens the mail composer modal to write and send new outbound emails.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Folder Directory" icon="folder-tree" color="#10B981">
    - **Sent**: Access sent messages and outgoing delivery logs.
    - **Inbox**: Primary incoming mail queue for unread and read customer threads.
    - **Important**: Messages flagged as high-priority or auto-categorized by system filters.
    - **Trash**: Deleted messages and archived junk.
    - **Draft**: Unsent email drafts saved for later editing.
    - **Spam**: Automatically filtered junk or suspicious emails.
    - **Starred**: Fast-access list for manually starred messages.
    - **Unread**: Filtered view displaying only unread incoming messages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Email List & Reader View

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Email List Pane" icon="list" color="#8B5CF6">
    - **Search Emails**: Real-time search bar to filter emails by sender name, subject header, or content keywords.
    - **Email Item Cards**: Lists messages with sender information, subject snippet, timestamp, and attachment indicators (paperclip icon).
    - **Load More**: Paginated loader to fetch historical email threads.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Mail Reading Pane" icon="book-open" color="#F59E0B">
    - **Message Viewer**: Displays the selected email thread, sender details, timestamps, body text, and embedded attachments. Select any email from the list pane to view its contents in full.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

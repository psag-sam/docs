<Note>
  **Overview**: Payment Templates establish standardized payment policies—such as full payment upfront or split deposit terms that can be attached to packages or used during order checkout. Because Payment Templates operate independently of package records, they are configured within the foundational catalog layer and can be reused across all travel offerings.
</Note>

## Data Table Structure

The Payment Templates table organizes commercial structures across standard operational parameters:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payment Templates Table Attributes" icon="table">
    - **Name** `String` — Unique identifier or descriptive label for the billing schedule (e.g., _10 % deposit_, _Pay your way!_, _Secured your booking and pay remaning balance on due date_, _PT-Full-Payment_).
    - **Payment Type** `Category` — Billing classification governing how customer charges are structured (_Deposit + Final Balance_, _Deposit + Installments_, _Full Payment_).
    - **Deposit %** `Numeric` — Required initial deposit percentage at checkout (e.g., _10.00%_, _5.00%_, _50.00%_, or _-_ for full payment).
    - **Status** `Status Badge` — Operational state indicated by a color-coded badge (_active_, _draft_, or _inactive_).
    - **Actions** `Row Menu` — Row menu controls allowing agents to **Edit (Pencil Icon)** or **Delete (Trash Icon)** the payment template.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

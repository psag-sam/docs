Once a Lead is created, clicking on their name in the dashboard opens their **Lead Details** page. This is where you actually "work" the lead.

![Image](/images/image-3.png "Image")

## The Visual Pipeline

At the very top of the page, you'll see a progress bar. This visually shows where the prospect currently is in your sales cycle: **New ➔ In Progress ➔ Qualified ➔ Disqualified**.

## Information Panels

The main body of the page displays all the data you collected during creation.

![Image](/images/image-4.png "Image")

- **Basic & Additional Details:** Quick access to phone numbers, emails, and company info.
- **Audit Information:** At the bottom, you can track exactly who created this record and when it was last updated.

## The Action Sidebar (Right Panel)

The right side of the screen is your toolkit for engaging with the prospect.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Activities" icon="calendar-check">
    Click **Add Activity** to schedule your next phone call, meeting, or task. This ensures you never forget to follow up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Echo Feed" icon="messages">
    Need help from a manager? Use the Echo Feed to type a message. Type `@Name` to tag a colleague, and they will get a notification to look at this lead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Attachments" icon="paperclip">
    Upload important documents like business cards, initial requirements, or NDAs directly to the lead's profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Calls & Recordings" icon="phone-volume">
    If your Pearl CRM has Twilio integration active, your native call logs and recordings with this prospect will appear here.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

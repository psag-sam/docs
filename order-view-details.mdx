## Primary Traveler Form Breakdown

Complete all mandatory fields (marked with a red asterisk `*`) for **Traveler 1 (Primary)** to establish the main contact for this reservation:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Personal Profile" icon="user">
    - **First name (as per passport) \***: Enter the traveler's given first name matching official government documents.
    - **Middle name (as per passport)**: Optional middle name.
    - **Last name (as per passport) \***: Enter the official surname.
    - **Date of birth \***: Select travel birthdate using the calendar picker (`DD/MM/YYYY`).
    - **Gender \***: Choose Gender from the dropdown options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact & Citizenship" icon="address-card">
    - **Nationality \***: Select citizenship country from the searchable dropdown menu.
    - **Email \***: Provide a valid email address to send digital vouchers and itineraries.
    - **Phone \***: Select country code (e.g., `+91`) and enter primary contact phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Passport Identification" icon="passport">
    - **Passport number**: Official passport identifier.
    - **Passport issue date & Passport expiry**: Issue and expiration dates (`DD/MM/YYYY`).
    - **Passport location**: City or issuing authority location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address & Special Requirements" icon="location-dot">
    - **Country \* & Address \***: Primary mailing location for billing and correspondence.
    - **Special details**: Text area (up to 4,000 characters) to log dietary preferences, wheelchair assistance, nut allergies, or early check-in requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Group Bookings & Actions

If booking for multiple individuals:

- **\+ ADD ANOTHER TRAVELER**: Click this primary button at the bottom of the form to append subsequent traveler fields (e.g., Traveler 2, Traveler 3).
- **BACK / NEXT**: Click **BACK** to adjust Step 1 selections, or click **NEXT: TRIP CUSTOMIZATION** $$\rightarrow$$ to proceed to Step 3.

## Order Summary Sidebar

The sidebar on the right maintains real-time tracking of:

- Package Name & Destination
- Travel Dates & Trip Duration
- Base Price calculation per traveler
- Tax Breakdown (Subtotal, VAT @ 20.00%, GST @ 15.00%)
- Departure City & Final Total Amount

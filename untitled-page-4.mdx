---
title: "Creating a New Account"
description: "Add a Pearl CRM account by entering basic and financial details, contact and tax info, billing and shipping addresses, and an account owner."
---

When a lead is qualified, or when you partner with a new vendor, you need to create an Account. Click the primary **\+ ADD ACCOUNT** button at the top-right corner of the dashboard to launch the creation form.

<Info>
  **Pro Tip:** Gather the company's tax identification and billing details before creating the account to ensure compliance and accurate invoicing later.
</Info>

![Image (14)](/images/image-\(14\).png "Image (14)")

## Step-by-Step Account Creation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic & Financial Information" icon="building">
    Start by entering the core company details:

    - **Account Name (Required):** The full legal or trading name of the organization.
    - **Account Type & Status:** Select if they are a Company, Partner, or Vendor, and set them as Active or Inactive.
    - **Industry & Website:** Select their market sector and enter their official URL.
    - **Financials:** Enter the **Annual Revenue**, **Number of Employees**, and select the operating **Currency** (e.g., US Dollar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact & Tax Details" icon="address-book">
    Provide the primary organizational contact channels:

    - **Communications:** Enter the main **Email**, corporate **Phone**, direct **Mobile Number**, and **Fax**.
    - **Tax ID:** Enter the company's tax identification or registration number. This is critical for billing and compliance verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Billing & Shipping Addresses" icon="truck">
    Accurate addresses ensure smooth invoicing and deliveries:

    - **Billing Address:** Enter the Street Address, City, State, Billing State Code (for tax calculations), Country, and Postal Code used for invoicing.
    - **Shipping Address:** If the delivery location is the same as the billing address, simply check the **"Shipping Address same as Billing Address"** box. Otherwise, fill in the shipping street, city, state, Shipping State Code (for routing), country, and postal code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ownership & Internal Notes" icon="user-lock">
    - **Account Owner:** Use the dropdown to assign the responsible account manager from your registered system users.
    - **Notes:** Add any internal remarks, account history, or special handling instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saving the Account

Once you have reviewed the information, click **SAVE ACCOUNT**. The system will validate your inputs and add the new account to your database catalog. _(If you need to discard the entry, click **CANCEL**)._

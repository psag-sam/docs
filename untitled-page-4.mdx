The **Merchant Asset Details** view provides comprehensive management over individual asset attributes, location metrics, and slot-based inventory allocations in **Pearl CRM**.

![Image](/images/image-42.png "Image")

<Note>
  **Asset Management**: Registering specific inventory items allows operators to manage capacities, baseline costs, and real-time date/slot availability before attaching assets to active travel itineraries.
</Note>

---

## Asset Overview & Attribute Specifications

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Basic Information" icon="info">
    - **Asset Name** `String` — Name or title of the merchant asset (e.g., _45-Min Coastal Helicopter Slot_).
    - **Asset Type** `Category` — High-level operational category (e.g., _Activity_).
    - **Trip Logistics** `Hyperlink` — Associated trip logistics package or service title (e.g., _Amalfi Aero-Prestige: Private Coastal Helicopter Flight_).
    - **Inventory Controlled** `Boolean` — Indicator (`Yes`/`No`) specifying if inventory count is managed automatically.
    - **Merchant** `User Link` — Assigned merchant partner or ID (e.g., `#80`).
    - **Subtype** `Category` — Specialized asset classification (e.g., _guided tour_).
    - **Status** `Status Badge` — Operational availability state (_Active_).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Details & Location" icon="location-dot">
    - **Capacity Model** `Category` — Passenger or unit capacity model (e.g., _Per Pax_).
    - **Maximum Capacity per Unit** `Numeric` — Maximum allowed limit per unit.
    - **Cost Price** `Numeric` — Standard unit cost price (e.g., `900`).
    - **Lead Time (Hours)** `Numeric` — Advance booking notice required in hours.
    - **Cutoff (Hours)** `Numeric` — Operational booking cutoff lead time in hours.
    - **Rating** `Numeric` — Quality rating classification score.
    - **Geo Location** `String` — Primary operating venue address (e.g., _Damecuta Anacapri Helipad, Anacapri, Naples, Italy_).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Audit Information" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    - **Created By** `User Link` — Team member who generated the asset record (e.g., _Kunal Mehra_).
    - **Created Date** `Timestamp` — Initial record creation date and time (e.g., _Oct 6, 2026, 04:50 PM_).
    - **Modified By** `User Link` — User who performed the latest configuration edit (e.g., _Shobhit Kumar_).
    - **Modified Date** `Timestamp` — Date and timestamp of the last system update (e.g., _Oct 6, 2026, 11:07 PM_).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Merchant Asset Inventory Allocation

The **Merchant Asset Inventory** sidebar card manages date-specific slot capacity and pricing overrides for the active resource.

<Card title="Merchant Asset Inventory Panel" color="#3B82F6" icon="box-archive">
  Click **New** on the panel header to launch the inventory modal overlay and configure scheduled date or slot availability.
</Card>

## Adding Merchant Asset Inventory

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch Inventory Modal">
    Click the **New** button inside the **Merchant Asset Inventory** panel on the asset details screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Schedule & Capacity">
    Populate the required slot parameters:

    - **Merchant Asset** `*` `Dropdown (Required)` — Automatically set to the current asset record (e.g., _45-Min Coastal Helicopter Slot (#69)_).
    - **Departure Date** `*` `Date Picker (Required)` — Select the target departure date (`mm/dd/yyyy`).
    - **Time Slot** `Time Picker` — Set the specific operational start time (`HH:mm`).
    - **Total Units** `*` `Numeric (Required)` — Total inventory units available for this date/time.
    - **Available Units** `Numeric` — Current unbooked units (defaults to Total Units).
    - **Total Capacity** `*` `Numeric (Required)` — Maximum passenger capacity for the allocated slot.
    - **Available Capacity** `Numeric` — Unbooked passenger capacity remaining.
    - **Status** `*` `Dropdown (Required)` — Set operational slot state (_Available_).
    - **Occupancy Status** `*` `Dropdown (Required)` — Select current fill status (_Empty_).
    - **Override Price** `Numeric` — Optional custom price override for this specific slot.
    - **Mark Unavailable (Blackout)** `Toggle` — Enable to blackout and lock the slot from further bookings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Inventory Allocation">
    Click **Save Inventory** to submit the record and link the active inventory slot to the merchant asset profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

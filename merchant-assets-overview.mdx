Centralizing assets allows sales and operations teams to monitor inventory availability, vendor links, location parameters, and guest capacity limits across all offerings.
</Note>

## Data Table Structure

The Merchant Assets table organizes inventory metrics across standard operational columns:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Merchant Assets Table Attributes" icon="table">
    - **Asset Name** `String` — Identifier of the vendor inventory item (e.g., _Chauffeur Sedan_, _Himalayan View Hotel - Deluxe Double Room_).
    - **Asset Type** `Category` — Operational category of the asset (_Transport_, _Accommodation_, _Activity_).
    - **Merchant** `User Link` — Partner company or vendor owning or operating the asset (e.g., _Airport Transfers & Chauffeur Services_, _Himalayan Stay Partners_).
    - **Max Cap / Unit** `Numeric` — Maximum passenger or guest allowance per single unit (e.g., 2 for a sedan, 8 for a shared dorm).
    - **Geo Location** `String` — Operating city or regional location (e.g., _Noida_, _Rome_, _Amalfi Coast_, _Rishikesh_).
    - **Status** `Status Badge` — Operational availability state indicated by a badge (_Active_ or _Inactive_).
    - **Modified Date** `Timestamp` — Timestamp of the last configuration update.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

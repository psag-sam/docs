In **Pearl CRM**, suppliers such as hotels, transport providers, and activity vendors are registered directly within the **Accounts** directory under designated partner classifications.

## Merchants & Accounts Master Table

The central table consolidates client and vendor profiles with the following attributes:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Merchants & Accounts Master Table Attributes" icon="table">
    - **NAME** `Hyperlink` — Official company or trading entity title (e.g., _Tbo Travel Partner_, _Kb New Account_, _Tpo Service_).
    - **ACCOUNT TYPE** `Category` — Core account category (e.g., _Partner_, _Company_).
    - **PARTNER TYPE** `Category` — Specialized classification designating supplier roles (e.g., _Merchant_).
    - **SERVICE TYPE** `Category` — Specific vendor operational capability (e.g., _DMC_, _Activity_, _Accommodation_).
    - **COUNTRY** `String` — Primary country of corporate registration (e.g., _India_, _United States_, _Algeria_).
    - **INDUSTRY** `Category` — Industry sector classification (e.g., _Travel_, _Construction_, _Education_).
    - **ACCOUNT STATUS** `Status Badge` — Operational state indicator (_Active_).
    - **ANNUAL REVENUE & CURRENCY** `Numeric / Code` — Recorded annual financial scope and standard transaction currency (e.g., _INR 2000_, _INR 150000_).
    - **OWNER** `User Link` — Assigned sales manager or account administrator (e.g., _Shivam Malhotra_, _Sarthak Naulakha_, _Kunal Mahra_).
    - **MODIFIED DATE** `Timestamp` — Timestamp tracking the most recent account profile update.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Merchant Management Workflow

Merchant accounts serve as the baseline foundation for all vendor logistics and asset management in **Pearl CRM**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account Registration">
    Suppliers are added via **\+ Add Account** by setting **Account Type** to _Partner_ and **Partner Type** to _Merchant_.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Asset Linking">
    Registered merchants become selectable vendors when creating physical inventory items in **Merchant Assets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inventory Allocation">
    Contracted merchant inventory is allocated to trip departure dates through **Merchant Allocations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

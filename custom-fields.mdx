The **Custom Fields** tab (`/Settings/Company-Settings`) in **Pearl CRM** allows administrators to extend standard data schemas by adding custom fields to core CRM objects to capture business-specific data.

![Screenshot 2026 10 01 095853](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-01-095853.png "Screenshot 2026 10 01 095853")

## Overview

Custom fields can be added across multiple primary entities, enabling tailored data collection for leads, contacts, customer accounts, sales opportunities, and travel orders.

## Supported CRM Entities

Administrators can switch between entity tabs to manage object-specific custom fields:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Supported Objects Taxonomy" icon="layer-group" color="#3B82F6">
    - **Leads**: Inquiry and prospect qualification parameters.
    - **Contacts**: Individual traveler and customer profile fields.
    - **Deals**: Sales opportunity metrics and custom deal attributes.
    - **Accounts**: Corporate account and agency metadata.
    - **Orders**: Travel booking and fulfillment tracking attributes.
    - **Quotations**: Custom pricing and proposal variables.
    - **Products**: Inventory item metadata and service package specifications.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Active Custom Fields Ledger

Lists all existing custom fields configured for the selected entity:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ledger Column Specifications" icon="table" color="#10B981">
    - **Field Label** \* Text (Required) — Display label presented to agents on form layouts (e.g., `Temp Field`).
    - **Data Type** \* Dropdown (Required) — Variable format defining allowed inputs (`Text`, `Number`, `Dropdown`, `Date`).
    - **Required Toggle** \* Toggle (Optional) — Enforces mandatory entry during record creation or edit actions.
    - **Delete Button** \* Button (System) — Permanently removes the custom field and its associated stored values.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Adding a New Custom Field

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Entity Tab" icon="folder-tree">
    Navigate to the desired entity tab (e.g., **Leads**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate Builder Panel" icon="square-plus">
    Locate the **Add New Custom Field** builder panel at the bottom of the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Field Label" icon="pen-to-square">
    **Field Label**: Enter a unique title for the new input field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Data Type" icon="database">
    **Data Type**: Select input data constraints (e.g., `Text`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Validation Rules" icon="toggle-on">
    **Required Toggle**: Enable if the field must be completed before saving records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Custom Field" icon="floppy-disk">
    Click **Add Custom Field** to commit the new field to form layouts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Quota Tracking**: The quota usage counter (e.g., `1 of 10 custom fields used`) tracks field capacity per entity.
</Note>

---
title: "Account Details View"
---

Once an Account is created, clicking on its name in the dashboard opens the **Account Details** page. This page acts as your central hub for that specific business entity. Every interaction, deal, and support ticket related to this company is managed right here.

![Image](/images/image-5.png "Image")

## The Account Header & Quick Actions

At the very top of the profile, you will see a blue header with the Account Name (e.g., "John"). On the right side of this header, there are quick-action icons:

- **Call / Email:** Instantly reach out to the primary contact.
- **Edit:** Open the form to update any account details.
- **Copy/Clone:** Duplicate the record if needed.
- **Delete:** Remove the account from the system (requires proper permissions).

## Information Panels

The main body of the page is divided into clear sections so you can quickly find the data you need:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic Information">
    Displays the core identity of the business, including Account Type, Industry, Status (Active/Inactive), primary phone/email, Website, and their Tax ID. It also shows the assigned Account Owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Financial Information">
    A quick look at their scale, showing Annual Revenue, operating Currency (e.g., USD), and the Number of Employees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address Panels">
    Clearly separates the **Billing Address** (for invoices) and the **Shipping Address** (for deliveries).

    ![Image](/images/image-6.png "Image")
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **Custom Fields:** Any organization-specific fields will appear here.
- **Audit Information:** At the very bottom, you can track accountability. It shows exactly who created the record, who last modified it, and the exact timestamps.

## The Action & Relationship Sidebar (Right Panel)

The right-hand sidebar is the most powerful part of this page. It connects the Account to other modules in Pearl CRM.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Communication & Productivity" icon="comments">
    - **Activities:** Click **Add Activity** to schedule tasks, calls, or meetings related to this account.
    - **Attachments:** Upload contracts, NDAs, or tax documents directly to the profile.
    - **Calls & Recordings:** View logs of all telephony interactions.
    - **Echo Feed:** Tag your team members to leave internal notes or request help regarding this account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Linked Records (The CRM Ecosystem)" icon="link">
    This section links the company to actual people and revenue:

    - **Contacts:** Click **Add Contact** to link individual people (like a CEO or a Billing Manager) to this Account.
    - **Deals:** Click **Add Deal** to start a new sales opportunity with this company.
    - **Orders:** View or create active sales orders.
    - **Cases:** Click **Add Case** if the customer raises a support ticket or complaint.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

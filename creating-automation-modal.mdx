The **Create Automation** selection modal in **Pearl CRM** serves as the initial entry point when clicking **\+ Create Automation** from the main Automations directory workspace.

![Screenshot 2026 10 01 103219](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-01-103219.png "Screenshot 2026 10 01 103219")

## Overview

The modal presents administrators with two distinct paths for designing automated workflows: choosing pre-configured templates for rapid deployment or constructing a custom multi-step rule tailored to specific business logic.

<CardGroup cols="{2}">
  <Card title="Pre-Built Templates" color="#3B82F6" icon="layer-group">
    Deploy pre-configured automation templates for common business use cases like lead scoring and task assignment.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Custom Workflow Engine" color="#10B981" icon="sliders">
    Construct tailored multi-step automation rules using specific CRM triggers, conditions, and action sequences.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Creation Pathways

<CardGroup cols="{2}">
  <Card title="1. Use Template" color="#3B82F6" icon="file-code">
    - **Description**: Provides quick deployment using pre-built automation templates designed for common business use cases such as lead scoring, email automation, data sync, and task assignments.
    - **Category Tags Preview**: Highlights supported workflow categories including `Data Sync`, `Email Automation`, `Lead Scoring`, and `Task Assignment`.
    - **Primary Action**: Click **Browse Templates** to open the full template catalog overlay and select a ready-to-use template.
  </Card>

  <Card title="2. Build Custom" color="#10B981" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    - **Description**: Provides complete control to design custom automated workflows tailored to specific CRM triggers, complex multi-field conditions, and multi-step action sequences.
    - **Visual Indicator**: Displays the 4-step progress icon representing the step-by-step setup wizard (Trigger, Conditions, Actions, Review).
    - **Primary Action**: Click **Start Building** to launch Step 1 (Trigger Setup) of the custom automation builder.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The **Account Details View** in **Pearl CRM** serves as a 360-degree management hub for an individual client account or organization. It consolidates basic company profile data, shipping and billing addresses, financial metrics, custom fields, audit logs, and interactive operational widgets.

## Overview

The details screen is divided into two primary sections:

1. **Main Information Panel (Left)**: Displays structured profile data, address blocks, financial metrics, custom fields, and audit logs.
2. **Interactive Widget Sidebar (Right)**: Provides real-time feeds and quick-action tools for managing linked activities, files, conversations, internal feeds, contacts, deals, and support cases.

---

## Top Banner Quick Actions

Execute core account operations directly from the action icons located in the upper right corner of the main header card:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Change View / Access Control" icon="eye">
    Modify record visibility or portal access settings for the account.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Launch the email composer directly to contact account representatives.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Edit Record" icon="pen-to-square">
    Clicking **Edit Account** transforms the main information panel into inline editing mode, replacing header action icons with **Save** and **Cancel** controls.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Clone & Delete Options" icon="copy">
    - **Clone Record**: Duplicate account configuration settings for new entities.
    - **Delete Record**: Remove account profile from the Pearl CRM database.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Main Account Information Panel

Explore structured profile breakdown sections across the main panel:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1. Basic Information" icon="id-card">
    - **Name & Owner**: Legal business title and assigned internal team owner link (clicking opens user profile).
    - **Account Type & Industry**: Classification categories (e.g., `Company`, `Travel`).
    - **Account Status**: Record state indicator (`Active` or `Draft`).
    - **Website**: Corporate URL.
    - **Phone & Mobile**: Primary office telephone and direct mobile contact numbers.
    - **Email & Fax**: Official email address and fax number.
    - **Tax ID**: Official tax registration number.
    - **Notes**: Internal operational guidance and client preferences.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="2. Address Blocks" icon="location-dot">
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Shipping Address" icon="truck">
        - **Address**: Physical street address.
        - **City & State**: Geographic location.
        - **State Code & Postal Code**: Region code and postal ZIP code.
        - **Country**: Operating country.
      </Card>

      <Card title="Billing Address" icon="file-invoice">
        - **Address**: Registered billing street address.
        - **City & State**: Geographic billing location.
        - **State Code & Postal Code**: Billing region code and postal code.
        - **Country**: Registered country.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3. Financial Information" icon="wallet">
    - **Annual Revenue**: Registered commercial revenue scope.
    - **Currency**: Transaction currency setting.
    - **Number of Employees**: Total company size indicator.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4. Custom Fields & Audit Information" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    - **Custom Fields**: Extensible data fields configured for organization-specific workflows.
    - **Created By**: Indicates record creator and creation timestamp.
    - **Modified By**: Tracks last modifying team member and timestamp.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Right-Hand Side Activity Panel

The sidebar consolidates active touchpoints and downstream linked entities:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Activities" icon="calendar-check">
    ### Task & Meeting Management

    Tracks scheduled tasks, upcoming sales meetings, and follow-up reminders.

    - **Action**: Click **Add Activity** to open the task/meeting creation modal directly on the page.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Attachments" icon="paperclip">
    ### Document & File Storage

    Stores uploaded contracts, identity copies, travel itineraries, and official PDFs.

    - **Action**: Click **Add Attachment** to upload new files.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Echo Feed" icon="bullhorn">
    ### Internal Collaboration Feed

    Internal team discussion feed for posting comments and tagging account collaborators.

    - **Action**: Click **Add Message** to post an internal note.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Contacts" icon="users">
    ### Account Contacts & Representatives

    Lists individual contact persons registered under this account.

    - **Quick Preview & Navigation**: Hovering or clicking on a contact badge (e.g., `AW / Alexander`) opens a pop-up card containing contact details with quick-copy buttons. Clicking the contact's name navigates directly to the **Contact Details View** page (`/contacts/{id}`).
    - **Action**: Click **Add Contact** to open the pop-up modal requiring \*\*First Name \*\*\* and \*\*Last Name \*\*\* along with optional **Email** and **Phone** fields to directly link a new person.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Deals" icon="handshake">
    ### Pipeline Opportunities

    Summarizes active sales pipeline opportunities, contract values, closing dates, pipeline stages, and probability percentages.

    - **Navigation**: Clicking a deal card navigates directly to the dedicated **Deal Details View** page (`/deals/{id}`).
    - **Action**: Click **Add Deal** to launch a new deal pipeline record modal.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cases" icon="headset">
    ### Support & Service Tickets

    Displays open customer service tickets, inquiries, and support requests linked to the account.

    - **Navigation**: Clicking an existing case card opens its dedicated **Case Details View** page (`/cases/{id}`).
    - **Action**: Click **Add Case** to open the creation modal with fields for **Account** (pre-populated), **Contact**, **Module Selector**, **Assigned To**, **Status \***, **Internal Status**, **Email**, **Case Source \***, **Category \***, **Sub Category**, **Subject \***, and **Description \***.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

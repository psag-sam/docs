## Qualifying a Lead (Stage 3)

When a prospect is ready to purchase or move into formal contract negotiations, selecting **Stage 3 (Qualified)** on the pipeline stepper bar triggers the lead conversion workflow.

![Image](/images/image-40.png "Image")

## Lead Qualification Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account Setup">
    - **Create New Account**: Check this option to generate a brand new corporate profile.
    - **Select Account**: Alternatively, associate the contact with an existing company profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Creation">
    - **Create New Contact**: Checked by default to convert lead details into a permanent contact entry.
    - **First Name & Last Name**: Automatically populated from the lead record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deal Configuration">
    Configure the initial sales deal metrics:

    - **Deal Value**: Estimated revenue in default currency.
    - **Deal Stage**: Defaults to _Qualification_.
    - **Probability (%)**: Estimated win likelihood percentage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize Conversion & Automatic Redirect">
    Click **CONFIRM QUALIFICATION**. Pearl CRM creates all linked records and automatically redirects you directly to the new **Deal Details View**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Post-Qualification Results**:

  - Lead status updates to Qualified.
  - Linked entries are generated in Contacts and Deals.
  - The system redirects you straight to the active Deal Details View.
</Tip>

## Disqualifying a Lead (Stage 4)

If an inquiry is determined to be non-viable, invalid, or unresponsive, agents can close the opportunity by selecting **Stage 4 (Disqualified)** on the pipeline stepper bar.

![Image](/images/image-41.png "Image")

## How Disqualification Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Disqualified Stage">
    Click **Stage 4 (Disqualified)** directly on the top pipeline status stepper bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="System Status Update">
    Pearl CRM instantly updates the status indicator to **Disqualified** and displays a green notification confirmation in the lower-right corner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Key Characteristics of Disqualified Records**:

  - **Lead Status Badge**: Updates to Disqualified under Basic Information.
  - **Stepper Milestone**: Highlighted in red on the status bar.
  - **No Data Loss**: The prospect record remains stored in your database for future reporting and audit tracking without creating clutter in active Deals or Contacts.
</Note>

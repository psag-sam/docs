## Directory View Controls

Switch between workspace layouts using the primary view selector controls:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="My Leads">
    Filters the workspace to display exclusively travel leads assigned to your active account user.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="My Team's Leads">
    Displays all active travel inquiries across your assigned sales team members.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="List View">
    Presents travel leads in a dense tabular format for multi-attribute sorting and batch auditing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Kanban View">
    Visualizes travel inquiry pipelines by status columns for drag-and-drop stage progression.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Import & Record Creation Workflows

Use the upper toolbar buttons to ingest lead data into the CRM:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Import Template">
    Click **Template** to download `leads_template.csv`. Use this standard file layout to format external travel lead batches prior to upload. Ensure all compulsory fields—specifically **Last Name** and at least one contact channel (**Email** or **Phone**)—are pre-filled in the template columns to avoid import errors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute Bulk Upload">
    Click **Bulk Upload** to launch the native system file selector. Select your populated `.csv` template to import multiple travel lead records simultaneously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manual Record Registration">
    Click **\+ Add Lead** to open the single lead creation drawer and register a traveler manually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

<Warning>
  **Mandatory Field Validation**: Manual lead entry and template CSV imports require a **Last Name** value, alongside at least one valid contact detail (**Email** or **Phone**).
</Warning>

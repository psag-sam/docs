title: "Lead Detail View"
---

The **Lead Details View** in **Pearl CRM** serves as the central command console for managing an individual prospect record. It consolidates lead qualification status stages, core contact information, commercial scope, location data, audit trails, and real-time interaction widgets.

![Image](/images/image-25.png "Image")

## Qualification Stage Bar

Located directly beneath the header card, clicking any stage step circle updates the record instantly without intermediate modals or confirmation prompts:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stage 1: New" icon="circle-1">
    Default initial ingestion stage for newly created or inbound inquiries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage 2: In Progress" icon="circle-2">
    Active sales outreach, discovery calls, or follow-up communications under way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage 3: Qualified" icon="circle-3">
    Lead criteria validated and marked ready for conversion into downstream pipeline entities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage 4: Disqualified" icon="circle-4">
    Inactive lead state for unqualified or non-responsive inquiries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Instant Record Synchronization**: Clicking any stage circle instantly updates the **Lead Status** and refreshes the **Modified Date** timestamp under Audit Information without requiring manual page reloads.
</Note>

## Header Action Controls

Quickly execute primary record operations from the upper right header banner:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Edit Record" icon="pen-to-square">
    Transforms the page directly into inline editing mode, replacing action icons with **Save** and **Cancel** controls.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Compose Email" icon="envelope">
    Opens the **Compose Email** modal pre-populated with recipient details, CC/BCC toggles, rich-text editor, and attachment support.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Clone Record" icon="copy">
    Creates a duplicate lead with identical field details, automatically resetting the **Lead Status** to **New**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Delete Record" icon="trash-can">
    Permanently removes the lead profile from the CRM database and redirects to the main directory.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Main Information Panels

Explore the structured profile breakdown panels:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Additional Details & Financial Profile" icon="building-columns">
    - **Address & Location**: Captures street address, City, State, Country, and Postal Code.
    - **Financial Scope**: Stores registered estimated **Annual Revenue** and operational **Currency**.
    - **Headcount**: Stores estimated workforce size (**Number of Employees**).
    - **Notes**: Specific inquiry details and client requests recorded during intake.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Basic Contact & Attribution Profile" icon="id-card">
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Identity & Communication" icon="user">
        Captures **Salutation**, full prospect name, primary **Email**, **Phone**, and **Mobile** contact numbers.
      </Card>

      <Card title="Business Context" icon="globe">
        Stores company **Website**, primary **Industry**, and **Interested Packages**.
      </Card>

      <Card title="Pipeline & Rating" icon="chart-line">
        Tracks operational **Lead Status**, priority **Lead Rating**, **Lead Source**, and **Website Reference** attribution.
      </Card>

      <Card title="Assignment & Preference" icon="user-gear">
        Defines account **Type**, primary **Language**, and assigned account manager (**Assigned To**).
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Audit & Timestamp Trail" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    - **Created By**: Indicates record creator and creation timestamp.
    - **Modified By**: Tracks last modification author and timestamp.
    - **Last Activity**: Timestamp tracking the most recent sales touchpoint or logged interaction.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Interactive Activity Sidebar

The right-hand sidebar consolidates real-time feeds and quick-action tools for managing prospect touchpoints:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Activities" icon="calendar-check">
    ### Task & Meeting Management

    Tracks scheduled calls, discovery meetings, tasks, and follow-up reminders.

    - **Action**: Click **Add Activity** to open the creation modal.
    - **Configurable Parameters**: Set Activity Type (`Call`, `Meeting`, `Email`, `Task`), Title, Description, Due Date/Time, Activity Status (`Scheduled`, `Completed`, `Cancelled`), and Assignee.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Attachments" icon="paperclip">
    ### Document Storage

    Stores uploaded pitch decks, requirements documents, identity copies, and official PDFs.

    - **Action**: Click **Add Attachment** to launch the upload modal.
    - **Constraints**: Supports file browser or drag-and-drop uploads for all standard file formats up to a **5MB** size limit.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Conversations" icon="comments">
    ### Communication Logs

    Summarizes direct communication histories, including recorded telephone calls and integrated WhatsApp message threads linked directly to this lead record.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Echo Feed" icon="bullhorn">
    ### Internal Collaboration Feed

    Internal workspace feed for posting comments, tagging sales teammates, and logging status updates.

    - **Action**: Click **Add Message** to open the **Post a Feed** modal.
    - **Features**: Includes `@` mention support to tag team members directly in internal notes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

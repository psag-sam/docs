Creating an account in **Pearl CRM** registers a corporate client profile, agency partner, or merchant supplier within the platform. Accounts can be registered directly from the master **Accounts Overview** directory using the creation modal.

## Overview

When creating an account, you can store complete profile details, corporate communications, financial parameters, billing and shipping addresses, account ownership assignments, and operational notes.

---

## Account Creation Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch Creation Modal" icon="plus">
    Click **\+ Add Account** from the top right banner of the master Accounts directory to open the **Add New Account** modal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Business Identity & Details" icon="building">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Core Identity" icon="signature">
        <CardGroup cols={2}>
          <Card title="Account Name *" icon="font">
            Enter the legal organization title (**Mandatory Field**).
          </Card>

          <Card title="Account Classification" icon="sitemap">
            Select organizational classification (**Account Type**: `Company` or `Partner`) and market vertical (**Industry**).
          </Card>
        </CardGroup>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Financials & Scope" icon="chart-simple">
        <CardGroup cols={3}>
          <Card title="Account Status" icon="toggle-on">
            Set operational state (`Active` or `Draft`).
          </Card>

          <Card title="Financial Scope" icon="sack-dollar">
            Enter registered **Annual Revenue** and total **Number of Employees**.
          </Card>

          <Card title="Currency" icon="coins">
            Select primary transaction currency from the dropdown.
          </Card>
        </CardGroup>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Contact Channels" icon="address-book">
        <AccordionGroup>
          <Accordion title="Digital Contact Methods" icon="globe">
            - **Website**: Enter corporate web domain or portal URL.
            - **Email**: Enter primary corporate inquiry address.
          </Accordion>

          <Accordion title="Telecommunication Lines" icon="phone">
            - **Phone & Mobile Number**: Direct business lines and mobile contacts.
            - **Fax & Tax ID**: Official fax number and state/government tax registration ID.
          </Accordion>
        </AccordionGroup>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Addresses" icon="location-dot">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Billing Address" icon="file-invoice">
        Provide official billing location details:

        <CardGroup cols={2}>
          <Card title="Street Location" icon="road">
            Enter physical **Street Address**.
          </Card>

          <Card title="City & State" icon="city">
            Input geographic **City** and **State**.
          </Card>

          <Card title="Region & Country" icon="flag">
            Enter **Billing State Code** and choose **Country** from selection list.
          </Card>

          <Card title="Postal Identifier" icon="mail-bulk">
            Provide postal or ZIP code.
          </Card>
        </CardGroup>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Shipping Address" icon="truck-fast">
        <AccordionGroup>
          <Accordion title="Option A: Mirror Billing Location" icon="copy">
            Check **Shipping Address same as Billing Address** to automatically populate shipping location with billing inputs.
          </Accordion>

          <Accordion title="Option B: Custom Delivery Location" icon="pen">
            Manually enter distinct fulfillment fields: **Street Address**, **City**, **State**, **Shipping State Code**, **Country**, and **Postal Code**.
          </Accordion>
        </AccordionGroup>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Ownership & Finalize" icon="floppy-disk">
    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Management & Internal Notes" icon="user-gear">
        - **Account Owner**: Select assigned sales representative or account manager from the team directory dropdown.
        - **Notes**: Use the dedicated text area to log internal account preferences, service tier agreements, or onboarding instructions.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>

    <Note>
      **Record Redirection**: Clicking **Save Account** validates all inputs, registers the organization profile, closes the modal, and automatically redirects directly to the newly created account's **Account Details View**.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

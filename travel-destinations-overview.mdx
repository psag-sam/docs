Welcome to the **Travel Destinations** module! In Pearl CRM, a Travel Destination serves as the master parent record for a geographical region or country (such as India, Italy, or Georgia) under which individual tour packages are created and managed.

The Travel Destinations dashboard acts as your central directory for searching, filtering, creating, and exporting destination records.

## Accessing Travel Destinations

To open the list view, expand the **Travel** section in the left sidebar navigation menu and select **Travel Destinations**.

## Navigating the Destinations Directory

The list view allows you to search, filter, and audit destination records across your workspace:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter & Search Records" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Use the top search field to quickly locate destinations by **Country Name** or **Continent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export Offline Reports" icon="file-csv">
    Click the **CSV** icon at the top right of the table to download an offline spreadsheet file of your active destinations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Destination Profile" icon="folder-open">
    Click directly on any **Country Name** (e.g., _India_ or _Italy_) to open its full detail view and manage its associated packages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Understanding Destination Data Table Columns" icon="table">
    - **COUNTRY NAME:** Name of the country (click to open the full Destination Details page).
    - **CONTINENT:** Geographical continent associated with the country (e.g., Asia, Europe, Africa).
    - **PACKAGES:** Count badge displaying the total number of tour packages attached to this destination.
    - **STATUS:** Operational state badge (**Active** or **Draft**).
    - **CREATED & MODIFIED:** Audit timestamps showing when the record was initially created and last updated.
    - **ACTIONS:** Contextual menu icon on the right to quickly edit or review the record.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---
title: "Using Prebuilt Templates"
---

The **Prebuilt Automation Templates** modal in **Pearl CRM** provides a centralized catalog of ready-to-use workflow configurations. Using prebuilt templates allows administrators to deploy standardized operational rules rapidly across lead nurturing, pipeline management, invoicing, support escalations, and third-party webhooks without configuring rules from scratch.

![Screenshot 2026 10 01 102151](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-01-102151.png "Screenshot 2026 10 01 102151")

## Overview

Selecting a template automatically pre-populates default trigger objects, event conditions, and output actions into the automation wizard canvas. Users can deploy templates as provided or refine individual field rules during the review process.

## Template Catalog Reference

The template library organizes 12 pre-configured workflows across core business functions:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1. Lead & Engagement Templates" icon="user-plus" color="#3B82F6">
    - **New Lead Welcome Email**
      - **Category**: Lead nurture
      - **Description**: Dispatches an automated welcome email as soon as a new lead enters the CRM.
      - **Target Object**: `Lead` | **Action**: `Send Email`
    - **Lead Owner Follow-up Task**
      - **Category**: Sales productivity
      - **Description**: Automatically creates a follow-up task whenever a new lead is assigned to an account executive.
      - **Target Object**: `Lead` | **Action**: `Create Task`
    - **Qualified Lead Alert**
      - **Category**: Lead management
      - **Description**: Generates a priority follow-up task when a lead status changes to qualified.
      - **Target Object**: `Lead` | **Action**: `Create Task`
    - **New Contact Welcome**
      - **Category**: Engagement
      - **Description**: Sends an automated welcome email message to newly registered contact profiles.
      - **Target Object**: `Contact` | **Action**: `Send Email`
    - **Inactive Lead Re-engagement**
      - **Category**: Lead nurture
      - **Description**: Automatically schedules a re-engagement task when a lead record is marked inactive.
      - **Target Object**: `Lead` | **Action**: `Create Task`
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="2. Deal & Pipeline Templates" icon="chart-line" color="#10B981">
    - **Deal Stage Follow-up**
      - **Category**: Pipeline
      - **Description**: Automatically schedules follow-up tasks whenever a deal transitions to a new stage in the sales pipeline.
      - **Target Object**: `Deal` | **Action**: `Create Task`
    - **Won Deal Onboarding**
      - **Category**: Customer success
      - **Description**: Initiates customer onboarding sequences and task assignments as soon as a deal is marked Closed Won.
      - **Target Object**: `Deal` | **Actions**: `Create Task`, `Send Email`
    - **High-value Deal Alert**
      - **Category**: Pipeline
      - **Description**: Creates an urgent priority review task whenever a large opportunity value threshold is recorded.
      - **Target Object**: `Deal` | **Action**: `Create Task`
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3. Orders, Invoicing & Support Templates" icon="file-invoice-dollar" color="#8B5CF6">
    - **Overdue Invoice Reminder**
      - **Category**: Finance
      - **Description**: Dispatches automated email payment reminders when an unpaid invoice passes its specified due date.
      - **Target Object**: `Invoice` | **Action**: `Send Email`
    - **Order Confirmation**
      - **Category**: Orders
      - **Description**: Dispatches an official order confirmation message as soon as a new order record is created.
      - **Target Object**: `Order` | **Action**: `Send Email`
    - **Case Escalation**
      - **Category**: Support
      - **Description**: Creates high-priority tasks and dispatches notification emails when a support case status escalates.
      - **Target Object**: `Case` | **Actions**: `Create Task`, `Send Email`
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4. Integration Templates" icon="webhook" color="#F59E0B">
    - **New Record Webhook**
      - **Category**: Integrations
      - **Description**: Transmits newly created lead data payloads automatically to a connected external web service or endpoint.
      - **Target Object**: `Lead` | **Action**: `Webhook`
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Deployment Procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch Automation Builder" icon="square-plus">
    Click **\+ Create Automation** from the Automations directory header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Template Catalog" icon="folder-open">
    Select **Use Template** and click **Browse Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter & Select Template" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Scroll through the template library or filter by category tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Populate Wizard Canvas" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Click **Use this template** on the chosen template card to populate the wizard with prebuilt triggers and actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & Save" icon="floppy-disk">
    Complete or adjust any default template parameters (e.g., target email templates or assigned task owners), then click **Save & Deploy**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

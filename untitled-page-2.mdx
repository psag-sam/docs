title: "Lead Status & Conversion"
description: "Update a lead through New, In Progress, and Qualified stages, then convert a qualified lead into linked Account, Contact, and Deal records."
---

As you call, email, and meet with a Lead, you must update their **Status** to reflect reality.

## Updating the Lead Status

You can update the status directly from the Lead Details page:

- **New:** The prospect was just added and hasn't been contacted yet.
- **In Progress:** You have started talking to them, sent an email, or are actively evaluating their needs.
- **Qualified:** The prospect is genuinely interested, has the budget, and is ready to discuss pricing or proposals!

## Converting a Qualified Lead

When a prospect reaches the **Qualified** stage, they should no longer be a "Lead". In Pearl CRM, you convert them into formal customer records to begin the actual sales negotiation.

When you change a Lead's status to **Qualified**, the system will automatically prompt you to convert them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm Account Details" icon="building">
    The system asks for an **Account Name**. This is the company or business entity the prospect belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Contact Details" icon="user">
    The prospect's First and Last Name will automatically transfer over to create their permanent **Contact** profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up the Deal" icon="handshake">
    A **Deal** represents the actual money on the table. Enter the estimated **Deal Value**, select the starting **Deal Stage** (like Needs Analysis or Proposal), and set the **Winning Probability** (e.g., 40%).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Convert" icon="check">
    Click **Save/Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **What happens next?** The Lead record is now closed. You will continue working with this customer using the **Accounts**, **Contacts**, and **Deals** tabs in your left sidebar!
</Info>

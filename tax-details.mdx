The **Tax Details** tab (`/Settings/Company-Settings`) in **Pearl CRM** provides centralized management for tenant-wide tax rules, active tax percentages, and financial billing compliance settings.

![Screenshot 2026 10 01 100016](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-01-100016.png "Screenshot 2026 10 01 100016")

## Overview

Configuring tax parameters ensures accurate calculation of VAT, GST, and regional tax obligations across generated invoices, quotations, and travel booking orders.

## Active Tax Directory Table

The main tax table lists all registered tax rules configured for the organization:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Directory Table Columns & Controls" icon="table" color="#3B82F6">
    - **Search Bar**: Quickly locate specific tax rules by name (e.g., `Search tax...`).
    - **TAX TYPE** \* Text (Required) — Name or classification of the tax rule (e.g., `VAT`, `GST`).
    - **RATE (%)** \* Percentage & Number (Required) — Applied percentage calculation rate (e.g., `20.00%`, `15.00%`).
    - **STATUS** \* Status (System) — Operational status badge showing whether the tax rule is currently applied to transactions (e.g., `Active`).
    - **ACTIONS** \* Quick Controls (System) — Quick controls for existing tax entries:
      - **Edit (Pencil Icon)**: Modify the rate percentage or tax rule name.
      - **Delete (Trash Icon)**: Remove the tax configuration from active selection.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Adding a New Tax Rule

To register a new tax configuration:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate Form Panel" icon="square-plus">
    Locate the **Add New Tax** form panel on the right side of the **Tax Details** workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Tax Type" icon="pen-to-square">
    **Tax Type** \*: Enter the legal name or abbreviation for the tax (e.g., `GST`, `VAT`, `Sales Tax`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Rate Percentage" icon="percent">
    **Rate (%)** \*: Specify the default tax percentage rate (e.g., `4.7`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Active Status" icon="toggle-on">
    **Active Checkbox**: Check **Active** to make this tax rule available immediately on billing forms and calculations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Tax Rule" icon="floppy-disk">
    Click **Add Tax** to save the rule, or click **Reset** to clear input fields.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Creation Workflow

Follow these steps to manually register a new prospective client:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch the Lead Modal">
    From the main **Leads** directory toolbar, click the **\+ Add Lead** button to open the creation modal form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Personal & Contact Details">
    Fill in primary contact information:

    - **Salutation**: Select a formal greeting (`Mr.`, `Ms.`, `Mrs.`, `Dr.`).
    - **First Name & Last Name**: Enter given name and family name.
    - **Email & Phone / Mobile**: Provide contact channels using international format for phone numbers (e.g., `+1234567890`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Specify Address & Location">
    Record primary location details including **Address**, **Country**, **State**, **City**, and **Postal Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Business & Financial Attributes">
    Capture commercial context:

    - **Website & Industry**: Corporate web URL and market sector classification.
    - **Annual Revenue & Headcount**: Set annual sales volume and number of employees.
    - **Currency**: Select preferred operational currency (defaults to `Australia (AUD)`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Pipeline Status & Attribution">
    Configure qualification parameters:

    - **Notes**: Add detailed inquiry context or specific client requirements.
    - **Lead Source & Status**: Set acquisition origin (`Website`, `Referral`) and operational status (defaults to `New`).
    - **Lead Rating & Trip**: Assign priority rank (`Hot`, `Warm`, `Cold`) and trip association.
    - **Language & Owner**: Choose primary language (defaults to `English`) and assign team ownership.
    - **Interested Packages**: Select relevant catalog product or package offerings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Record">
    Click **Save Lead**. Upon successful submission, the modal closes and redirects automatically to the newly created Lead Detail Record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Mandatory Validation Requirements:** The creation modal enforces data integrity. A **Last Name** entry is mandatory, alongside at least one valid contact channel (**Email** or **Phone**). Attempting to save without these fields triggers inline error warnings.
</Warning>

## Form Field Reference

Explore the fields available inside the lead creation form:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Contact & Identity Fields" icon="address-card">
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Salutation & Names" icon="user">
        Captures title, given name, and required family name (**Last Name** \*).
      </Card>

      <Card title="Communication Channels" icon="phone">
        Requires either primary **Email** \* or direct **Phone** \* / **Mobile** numbers.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Address Parameters" icon="location-dot">
    - **Street Address**: Primary location line.
    - **Country**: Dropdown country selector.
    - **State / City / Postal Code**: Geographic region and postal routing codes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Commercial & Financial Profile" icon="building">
    - **Website**: Corporate web domain.
    - **Industry**: Market vertical picklist.
    - **Financial Metrics**: Operating **Currency**, **Annual Revenue**, and **Number of Employees** count.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pipeline & Attribution Setup" icon="sliders">
    - **Status & Rating**: Sets pipeline stage (`New`, `In Progress`, `Qualified`, `Disqualified`) and lead temperature rating.
    - **Source & Reference**: Identifies ingestion origin and referring URL channels.
    - **Assignment & Packages**: Designates account owner, language preference, and interested service packages.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Agency Custom Fields" icon="cube">
    Supports workspace-specific custom parameters configured by your agency administrator (e.g., custom required inputs).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

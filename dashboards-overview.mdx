The **Dashboards** module in **Pearl CRM** centralizes key performance indicators, operational metrics, and reporting charts into customizable visual grids, providing real-time visibility across sales pipelines, revenue streams, service tickets, and team workloads.

![Screenshot 2026 09 30 110809](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-30-110809.png "Screenshot 2026 09 30 110809")

## Overview

The Dashboards directory organizes built-in and custom dashboard layouts, enabling agents, managers, and executives to monitor performance metrics at a glance or pin a preferred dashboard as their CRM homepage.

## Directory Controls & Toolbar

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Toolbar Actions & Search" icon="sliders" color="#3B82F6">
    - **Search Dashboards**: Dynamic search bar to filter dashboard cards by title or description keyword.
    - **\+ New Dashboard**: Opens the dashboard creation wizard to build custom widget layouts and pin charts from saved reports.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Dashboard Card Grid

The main dashboard workspace indexes accessible dashboard templates and custom layouts through structured cards featuring key metadata:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Dashboard Templates & Layouts" icon="border-all" color="#3B82F6">
    - **Business Overview**: Tracks organizational pipeline, total revenue, incoming leads, and service activities across the CRM (`8 widgets`, `Built-in`).
    - **My Travel Work**: Dedicated operational view monitoring traveler departures, installment payments, and booking statuses (`6 widgets`, `Built-in`).
    - **My Work**: Personal workspace focusing on individual pipelines, assigned tasks, and lead activities (`6 widgets`, `Built-in`).
    - **Overview**: General starting dashboard view configurable as a default home screen (`8 widgets`, `Built-in`).
    - **Team Overview**: Tracks team performance, agent workload distribution, and active items requiring attention (`7 widgets`, `Built-in`).
    - **Travel Overview**: High-level summary displaying active bookings, gross revenue, and service metrics at a glance (`8 widgets`, `Home`, `Built-in`).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Card Status Badges & Actions

<CardGroup cols="{2}">
  <Card title="Status Badges" color="#3B82F6" icon="tag">
    - **Home Badge (Green)**: Indicates which dashboard is currently set as the user's default homepage upon logging into Pearl CRM.
    - **Built-in Badge (Blue)**: Highlights default system dashboards pre-configured for standard CRM workflows.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Card Options" color="#10B981" icon="ellipsis-vertical">
    - **Card Options (Three Dots Menu)**: Options to **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Set as Home**, or **Delete** custom dashboards.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

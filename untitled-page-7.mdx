---
title: "Creating a New Contact"
---

Whenever you start communicating with a new individual at a company, you need to add them to the system. Click the **\+ ADD CONTACT** button on the dashboard to open the creation form.

<Info>
  **Important:** A contact profile must be associated with a valid **Account** to ensure data consistency across your CRM. Fields marked with a red asterisk (\*) are required.
</Info>

## Step-by-Step Creation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Personal Identifiers" icon="user">
    Start with the individual's core details:

    - **Name:** Select a Salutation (e.g., Mr., Dr.), and enter their **First Name** (_), Middle Name, and **Last Name** (_).
    - **Contact Info:** Provide their **Email** (_) and **Phone** (_) number. You can also add a direct Mobile Number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organization & Account Mapping" icon="building-user">
    Link the person to their employer:

    - **Account (\*):** Click this dropdown to search and select the parent organization from your existing CRM accounts.
    - **Primary Contact:** Select Yes/No to indicate if this person is your main liaison for the account.
    - **Role Setup:** Enter their internal **Department** (e.g., Billing, IT) and their specific **Contact Role** (\*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address & Status" icon="map-location-dot">
    - **Mailing Address:** Enter their physical correspondence address, including City, State, Country, and Postal Code.
    - **Status & Notes:** Set the Contact Status (Active/Inactive) and use the **Notes** box to record their background context or personal preferences (e.g., "Prefers morning calls").
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saving the Contact

Review the form to ensure all mandatory inputs are filled. Click **SAVE CONTACT** to commit the new profile to the database, or click **CLOSE** to dismiss the window without saving.

Welcome to the **Accounts** module! In Pearl CRM, an Account represents a confirmed business entity. This can include your customer organizations, partner entities, vendors, and client profiles.

## Accessing the Dashboard

To open the dashboard, go to the left sidebar navigation menu, look under the **SALES** section, and click on **Accounts**.

![Image (13) 1](/images/image-\(13\)-1.png "Image (13) 1")

## Performance Metrics (KPIs)

At the top of the Accounts dashboard, you will find four key metric cards that give you a quick snapshot of your database:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Total & Active Accounts" icon="building">
    **Total Accounts:** The complete count of registered accounts (with % growth vs. last month).<br />**Active Accounts:** The count of currently active accounts (with % change vs. last week).
  </Card>

  <Card title="Growth & New Accounts" icon="chart-line">
    **Account Growth:** The percentage growth rate of new accounts over the last quarter.<br />**New Accounts:** The number of newly added accounts (with % comparison against last week).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Navigating the Accounts Table

Below the KPIs is your central database table. You can use the header controls to find exactly what you need:

- **Search & Filter:** Use the real-time search box to locate accounts by name or attributes. Click the filter or bookmark icons for advanced sorting.
- **Page Size Selector:** Adjust the number of entries displayed per page (e.g., 10 entries per page).
- **CSV Export:** Click the **CSV** button to export your filtered list directly to a spreadsheet.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Understanding the Table Columns" icon="table">
    - **NAME:** The business or company name (click to view their full profile).
    - **ACCOUNT TYPE:** Category of the account (e.g., Partner, Customer, Vendor).
    - **PARTNER / SERVICE TYPE:** Specific classifications (e.g., Merchant, Guide, Accommodation).
    - **COUNTRY & INDUSTRY:** The location and market sector of the business.
    - **ACCOUNT STATUS:** Operational status indicated by a badge (Green for Active, Red for Inactive).
    - **ANNUAL REVENUE & CURRENCY:** Financial metrics and operating currency.
    - **OWNER:** The assigned account manager.
    - **MODIFIED DATE:** Timestamp of the last profile edit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

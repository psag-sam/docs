The **Leads** module is the starting point of the customer acquisition process in Pearl CRM. A Lead represents a prospective customer or business opportunity that has entered the sales pipeline but has not yet been converted into a formal customer relationship.

Pearl CRM lets you capture Lead information, assign ownership, track qualification progress, evaluate potential, and convert qualified Leads into the next stage of the customer lifecycle.

{/*  IMAGE: Leads module overview / listing page  */}

## What is a Lead?

A **Lead** is an initial prospect who may have an interest in the products or services offered by your organization. Leads are managed separately from confirmed customer records until they are sufficiently qualified, so the sales team can evaluate prospects before moving them into the customer and opportunity lifecycle.

<Accordion title="What a Lead record can contain" icon="id-card">
  - Personal information
  - Contact information
  - Address information
  - Communication preferences
  - Lead ownership
  - Lead status
  - Lead rating
  - Organization-specific information

  The Lead record acts as the central record for the prospect during the qualification stage.
</Accordion>

## Lead Lifecycle

A Lead progresses through defined stages before becoming part of the customer and opportunity lifecycle.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lead Capture" icon="inbox">
    A new prospect is entered into Pearl CRM with the available identity, contact, and preference information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="New" icon="sparkles">
    The Lead has been captured but has not yet entered active follow-up. Review the information, confirm ownership, and begin follow-up activities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In Progress" icon="spinner">
    The Lead is actively being followed up or evaluated. Continue communication, update Lead information, and assess against qualification criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualified" icon="badge-check">
    The Lead meets the required qualification criteria and is ready to move into the customer and opportunity lifecycle through conversion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account + Contact" icon="users">
    Conversion establishes the Account (customer organization) and Contact (individual person) records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deal" icon="handshake">
    A Deal is created to represent the active sales opportunity associated with the converted prospect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lead Management Process

The standard Lead management process in Pearl CRM:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the Lead" icon="plus">
    Create a Lead record and enter the available prospect information required for identification, communication, assignment, and qualification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign ownership" icon="user-check">
    Assign the Lead to the appropriate sales user. The assigned user becomes responsible for managing the Lead and progressing it through the sales process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and follow up" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Open the Lead record, review the customer information, and perform the required follow-up activities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Status and Rating" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Update the Lead Status as the prospect progresses, and set the Lead Rating to indicate the current sales potential.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualify the Lead" icon="badge-check">
    Once the prospect meets the required qualification criteria, update the Lead status to **Qualified**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert the Lead" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Start the Lead conversion process to move the prospect into the customer and opportunity lifecycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lead Assignment

Lead assignment determines which sales user is responsible for managing a Lead.

```text Assignment flow
Lead Created
     ↓
Assigned To
     ↓
Sales User
     ↓
Follow-up & Qualification
     ↓
Status Progression
```

A Lead is assigned to the user creating the record by default, unless the user's permissions and configured assignment rules allow assignment to another eligible user. The assignment model also affects record visibility and update access.

## Status vs Rating

Lead Status and Lead Rating are maintained independently. Status describes **where** the Lead is in the process; Rating describes **how promising** it is.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Lead Status">
    **Lead Status** represents where the prospect currently stands within the Lead lifecycle. Update the status as the Lead progresses through the sales process.

    | Status | Purpose |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | New | Lead has been captured and has not yet entered active follow-up or qualification |
    | In Progress | Lead is actively being followed up or evaluated |
    | Qualified | Lead has met the required qualification criteria and can proceed to conversion |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Lead Rating">
    **Lead Rating** indicates the current sales potential or temperature of the Lead.

    | Rating | Purpose |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | Hot | High current sales potential |
    | Warm | Moderate sales potential; requires continued engagement |
    | Cold | Lower immediate sales potential |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Comparison">
    | Field | Answers | Purpose |
    | :-- | :-- | :-- |
    | Status | Where is the Lead in the process? | Tracks Lead lifecycle progression |
    | Lead Rating | How promising is the Lead? | Indicates sales potential |

    <Tip>
      A Lead can be **In Progress** while having a **Hot**, **Warm**, or **Cold** rating. Maintain both fields independently according to the Lead's current situation.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Lead Conversion

Lead conversion is the transition from prospect management into the formal customer and opportunity lifecycle.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Account" icon="building">
    The customer organization or entity associated with the qualified Lead.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Contact" icon="user">
    The individual person associated with the customer Account.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Deal" icon="handshake">
    The active sales opportunity created or associated during conversion.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Conversion Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Qualify the Lead" icon="badge-check">
    Move the Lead to the **Qualified** status once it satisfies the organization's qualification criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start conversion" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Open the qualified Lead and select the conversion action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Account" icon="building">
    Provide or confirm the Account information that will represent the customer relationship.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Contact" icon="user">
    Review the Contact information associated with the Lead. The Lead's personal information can be used as the basis for the Contact record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Deal" icon="handshake">
    Configure the initial Deal information, including commercial value, Deal Stage, and Winning Probability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm conversion" icon="check">
    Review the conversion information and confirm the operation. Pearl CRM establishes the relevant customer and opportunity relationships, and the prospect can then be managed through the Account, Contact, and Deal modules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Conversion Relationship

The Lead module connects the initial prospecting process with the downstream sales modules, maintaining continuity between the original prospect record and the subsequent customer and sales records.

```text Lead conversion relationship
┌─────────────┐
│    LEAD     │
│  Prospect   │
└──────┬──────┘
       │
       │ Qualification
       ↓
┌─────────────┐
│   ACCOUNT   │
│  Customer   │
└──────┬──────┘
       │
       ├─────────────────┐
       ↓                 ↓
┌─────────────┐    ┌─────────────┐
│   CONTACT   │    │    DEAL     │
│    Person   │    │ Opportunity │
└─────────────┘    └─────────────┘
```

## Lead Access and Permissions

Lead access is controlled through Pearl CRM's role and ownership model. The Lead's ownership field is **Assigned To**.

<Info>
  Access control determines which Lead records a user can **view**, **create**, **update**, **manage**, and **reassign**. The applicable access level depends on the user's role, organizational hierarchy, and permissions.
</Info>

## Lead Management Rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ownership drives access" icon="user-lock">
    Each Lead has an assigned owner responsible for managing the record. The ownership field is used as part of Pearl CRM's access-control model.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Qualify before converting" icon="badge-check">
    A Lead should be moved to **Qualified** only after it satisfies the organization's qualification criteria. Lead conversion is intended for qualified prospects and establishes the relationship with the downstream Account, Contact, and Deal records.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Actions follow permissions" icon="shield-check">
    Users can only perform Lead actions permitted by their assigned role, ownership, and access permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep information current" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Lead information should be kept current throughout the qualification process so that downstream customer and opportunity records can be created or associated correctly during conversion.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

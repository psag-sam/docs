## Signing In to Your Workspace

Follow these steps to log in to Pearl CRM:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Login Page">
    Open your browser and navigate to your assigned Pearl CRM URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Credentials">
    - **Username / Email**: Enter your registered account email address (formatted as `username@agency`).
    - **Password**: Enter your secure account password. Click the eye icon (`👁️`) inside the password field to toggle text visibility between masked and plain text for verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Session Preferences">
    Check **Keep me signed in** if you are accessing the CRM on a secure, personal device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Click **Sign in** to access your workspace dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Resetting Your Password

If you cannot access your account or forget your password, follow these recovery steps:

1. Click **Forgot password?** located directly above the password input field to open the reset modal window.
2. Enter your registered `username@agency` in the input field.
3. Click **Send reset link** to dispatch a password recovery email to your address. Alternatively, click **Cancel** to close the popup and return to the main login form.

<Accordion title="Troubleshooting Login Issues">
  - **Password Reset Emails**: If you do not receive the reset link within a few minutes, check your email spam folder.
  - **Account Lockout / Access Requests**: Contact your agency administrator if you experience persistent login failures or need your account permissions updated.
</Accordion>

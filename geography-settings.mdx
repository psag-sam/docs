title: "Geography Settings"
---

The **Geography** tab (`/Settings/Company-Settings`) in **Pearl CRM** allows administrators to customize geography taxonomies, rename geographical entities, and toggle dropdown visibility across address fields throughout the workspace.

![Screenshot 2026 10 01 095721](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-01-095721.png "Screenshot 2026 10 01 095721")

<Note>
  **Overview**: Geography settings ensure global consistency when recording client addresses, flight destinations, and travel orders. Customizing display names or hiding non-operational regions streamlines data entry for agents.
</Note>

---

## Navigation Tabs

The Geography section is divided into three entity levels:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Countries">
    Manage global country master names, display labels, and visibility toggles.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="States">
    Configure state, province, or territorial region parameters per country.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cities">
    Manage city and municipality listings for address auto-completion.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

## Directory Table & Controls

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Geography Management Attributes & Controls" icon="sliders">
    - **Search Input** `Text Input` — Filter the geographical ledger by name keywords (e.g., `Search countries...`).
    - **MASTER NAME** `String` — The baseline system reference name for the geographical entity (e.g., _Afghanistan_, _Albania_, _Algeria_, _Andorra_, _Angola_, _Anguilla_).
    - **DISPLAY NAME** `Text Input` — Editable field allowing custom localized or rebranded display labels for your tenant workspace.
    - **HIDDEN Toggle** `Switch` — Toggle to hide or expose specific regions in system dropdowns across CRM forms:
      - **Off (Default)**: Region remains visible in address selection fields.
      - **On**: Region is hidden from selection dropdowns to prevent invalid address entries.
    - **SAVE (Disk Icon)** `Button` — Saves modified display names or toggle status updates for the individual row.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

The **Case Details** view (`/Cases/{id}`) in **Pearl CRM** serves as the central management workspace for tracking ticket progress, reviewing related customer accounts, managing internal notes, and updating case resolution lifecycle stages.

![Screenshot 2026 09 30 104806](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-30-104806.png "Screenshot 2026 09 30 104806")

## Overview

Opening an individual case record displays the complete ticket lifecycle, customer metadata, and internal collaboration tools organized across structured information panels.

<CardGroup cols="{2}">
  <Card title="Interactive Lifecycle Stepper" color="#3B82F6" icon="arrows-spin">
    Advance or revert ticket resolution states with single-click actions along an 8-stage visual progression bar.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sidebar Collaboration Tools" color="#10B981" icon="comments">
    Schedule follow-up tasks, upload diagnostic files, review call history, and post team notes via the Echo Feed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Interactive 8-Stage Lifecycle Status Stepper

The top banner features an interactive 8-stage visual progression bar that tracks ticket resolution states and allows agents to advance or revert case status with a single click:

<Steps>
  <Step title="New" icon="circle-dot">
    Initial ticket intake and registration upon creation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In Progress" icon="spinner">
    Support agent is actively investigating, processing, or working to resolve the issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On Hold" icon="circle-pause">
    Resolution is temporarily paused pending scheduled activities, internal escalation, or operational dependencies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Awaiting Customer Information" icon="user-clock">
    Resolution is blocked waiting for additional inputs, documentation, or clarification from the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Closed" icon="circle-check">
    The case has been successfully resolved, verified, and finalized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rejected" icon="circle-xmark">
    The ticket is determined to be invalid, duplicate, out-of-scope, or non-actionable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reopened" icon="rotate-left">
    A previously closed or rejected case is reactivated due to follow-up inquiries or recurring issues.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft" icon="file-pen">
    Unsubmitted, incomplete, or internal ticket draft retained for further review before official intake.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Note**: Clicking any numbered stage pill in the stepper immediately updates the case status field (e.g., transitioning from **New** to **In Progress**, **On Hold**, **Awaiting Customer Information**, or **Closed**) and displays a confirmation notification toast.
</Note>

## Action Controls

The top-right header banner provides administrative action controls:

<CardGroup cols="{2}">
  <Card title="Communication & Editing" color="#3B82F6" icon="envelope">
    - **Email (Mail Icon)**: Opens the integrated mail composer to send direct updates or requests to the customer's registered email address.
    - **Edit (Pencil Icon)**: Opens the case editing modal to modify ticket fields, priority levels, assigned agents, or descriptions.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Ticket Cloning" color="#10B981" icon="copy">
    - **Duplicate (Copy Icon)**: Clones the case attributes to log a related support ticket quickly.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Case Information Panels

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1. Basic Information" icon="info" color="#3B82F6">
    - **Case Number** \* Identifier (System) — System identifier code (e.g., `CASE-00018`).
    - **Title** \* Text (Required) — Primary ticket headline summarizing the inquiry (e.g., _Payment link generation issue for partial deposit_).
    - **Status** \* Status (System) — Active lifecycle state reflected in the stepper (`New`, `In Progress`, `On Hold`, `Awaiting Customer Information`, `Closed`, `Rejected`, `Reopened`, `Draft`).
    - **Internal Status** \* Status (System) — Operational sub-state for team tracking (`Pending Internal Team`, `Pending Third Party`, `Resolution in Progress`, `Ready for Closure`).
    - **Email** \* Text (Optional) — Primary contact email address (e.g., `s.jenkins@travela.com`).
    - **Priority** \* Dropdown (Required) — Assigned severity classification (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Urgent`).
    - **Case Source** \* Dropdown (Required) — Intake channel origin (`Email`, `Phone`, `Web Portal`).
    - **Category** \* Dropdown (Required) — Primary issue classification (e.g., `Billing & Payment`).
    - **Sub Category** \* Dropdown (Optional) — Specific issue taxonomy (e.g., `Payment Issue`).
    - **Description** \* Textarea (Required) — Comprehensive explanation of the customer request and system diagnostic details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="2. Related Information" icon="link" color="#10B981">
    - **Account** \* Text (Optional) — Linked corporate or agency profile (e.g., `TPO`).
    - **Contact** \* Text (Optional) — Primary traveler or account contact person (e.g., `Komal Bohara`).
    - **Assigned To** \* User (Required) — Support representative or owner managing ticket resolution (e.g., `Sarthak Naulakha`).
    - **Travel Order** \* Text (Optional) — Linked booking reference number (e.g., `215`).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3. Case Comments" icon="comments" color="#8B5CF6">
    Centralized internal collaboration feed:

    - Click **Add Comment** to log internal resolution notes, diagnostic updates, or escalation details for team members.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4. Audit Information" icon="shield-check" color="#F59E0B">
    - **Created By / Modified By** \* User (System) — User profile responsible for record creation and recent updates.
    - **Created Date / Modified Date** \* Date (System) — Precise system timestamps recording ticket history.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Sidebar Collaboration & Communications

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Collaboration Tools Breakdown" icon="bars-staggered" color="#6366F1">
    - **Activities**: Schedule follow-up tasks, log phone calls, or set calendar reminders using **Add Activity**.
    - **Attachments**: Upload supporting diagnostic files, error screenshots, or travel vouchers using **Add Attachment**.
    - **Conversations**: Review connected call logs or WhatsApp messaging history associated with the record.
    - **Echo Feed**: Post internal team updates, tag colleagues, or stream system activity notifications using **Add Message**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

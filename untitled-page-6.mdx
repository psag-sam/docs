## Lead Management Process

Pearl CRM follows a structured process for managing Leads.

 \<Steps> \<Step title="Capture the Prospect"> 

Create a Lead record when a new prospect enters the sales pipeline.

The Lead record stores the information required to identify and communicate with the prospect.

 \</Step> \<Step title="Assign Ownership"> 

The Lead is assigned to the appropriate sales user.

The assigned user becomes responsible for managing the Lead and progressing it through the qualification process.

 \</Step> \<Step title="Review and Follow Up"> 

Review the Lead information and perform the required sales follow-up.

Lead information can be updated as new information becomes available.

 \</Step> \<Step title="Track Lead Progress"> 

Update the Lead Status as the prospect progresses through the qualification process.

 \</Step> \<Step title="Evaluate Lead Potential"> 

Use Lead Rating to indicate the current sales potential or temperature of the Lead.

 \</Step> \<Step title="Qualify the Prospect"> 

When the Lead satisfies the organization's qualification criteria, move the Lead to the Qualified stage.

 \</Step> \<Step title="Convert the Lead"> 

A qualified Lead can be moved into the customer and opportunity lifecycle through the Lead Conversion process.

 \</Step> \</Steps>

Welcome to the **Contacts** module! In Pearl CRM, a Contact represents an individual person you interact with (like a CEO, Billing Manager, or Sales Rep) who is usually associated with a parent Account.

The Contacts Dashboard serves as your main directory for searching, filtering, creating, and exporting these records.

## KPI Metrics & Actions

At the top of your screen, you'll see your dashboard controls and a quick overview of your database.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Total Contacts" icon="users">
    Displays the aggregate count of all contacts registered in the system, along with a percentage variance compared to the previous month.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Data Controls" icon="sliders">
    Use the **Search Input Field** to locate specific records by name, email, or associated account. You can also adjust the **Entries Per Page** (e.g., 10, 25, or 50).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Navigating the Contacts Table

The main directory table gives you a bird's-eye view of your connections. You can perform quick actions directly from this list.

- **Export Data:** Click the **CSV** button in the header banner to export your filtered contact list to an offline spreadsheet file.
- **Create New:** Click the primary **\+ ADD CONTACT** button in the top-right to launch the creation modal.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Understanding List view Columns" icon="table">
    - **CONTACT NAME:** Full name of the contact (click this to open their complete profile).
    - **ACCOUNT:** Name of the associated company (click to jump to the parent Account profile).
    - **EMAIL & PHONE:** Primary contact channels.
    - **PRIMARY CONTACT:** A badge indicating if this individual is the main representative for their Account.
    - **STATUS:** Current lifecycle state (e.g., Draft, Active, Inactive).
    - **ACTION:** Contextual menu allowing you to quickly edit or delete the record.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

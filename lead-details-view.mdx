## Overview & Screen Layout

The record layout is organized into four main functional sections:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pipeline Stepper Bar" icon="bars-progress">
    Advance the prospect through four qualification stages: **New**, **In Progress**, **Qualified**, and **Disqualified**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Quick Action Toolbar" icon="screwdriver-wrench">
    Header icons on the top-right toolbar allow agents to **Edit** fields, **Send Email**, **Duplicate Record**, or **Delete Lead**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Profile Information Cards" icon="address-card">
    Central cards displaying **Additional Details** (location, revenue, notes), **Basic Information** (contact channels, assignment), and **Audit Information**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Engagement Sidebar" icon="sidebar">
    Right-hand side widgets for managing **Activities**, **Attachments**, **Conversations**, and internal team notes in **Echo Feed**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## Direct Record Actions

### Updating Pipeline Stage

Click on any numbered milestone in the top stepper bar to update the lead's active status in real time.

### Editing & Managing Fields

- **Edit (Pencil Icon):** Opens the complete lead modification modal to update personal, address, or business fields.
- **Email (Envelope Icon):** Opens the integrated email composer for direct outreach.
- **Duplicate (Copy Icon):** Creates a clone of the current lead entry.
- **Delete (Trash Icon):** Removes the record from the database.

---

## Right-Side Engagement Widgets

The right column keeps all interaction history organized alongside the core profile:

- **Activities:** Schedule follow-up calls, tasks, and meetings.
- **Attachments:** Upload and store travel itineraries, ID copies, or quote documents.
- **Conversations:** Review multi-channel messaging histories (such as WhatsApp or call logs).
- **Echo Feed:** Post internal updates and `@` mention team members for internal collaboration.

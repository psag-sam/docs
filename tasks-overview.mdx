The **Tasks** module in **Pearl CRM** provides a visual calendar workspace and task ledger for scheduling, filtering, and managing team activities and follow-up assignments.

![Screenshot 2026 09 30 105450](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-30-105450.png "Screenshot 2026 09 30 105450")

## Overview

The Tasks screen combines calendar views with task filtering, allowing agents and managers to keep track of upcoming commitments linked to accounts, cases, deals, or travel orders.

<CardGroup cols="{2}">
  <Card title="Interactive Calendar Workspace" color="#3B82F6" icon="calendar">
    Schedule, view, and manage team tasks directly across month, week, or day calendar grids.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Entity & Owner Filtering" color="#10B981" icon="filter">
    Filter activities by parent entity types (Accounts, Cases, Deals) or specific task owners.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Directory Filters & Controls

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Filter Parameters" icon="sliders" color="#3B82F6">
    - **Parent Object** \* Dropdown (Optional) — Filter calendar tasks by associated entity type (e.g., `Account`, `Case`, `Deal`).
    - **Owner** \* Dropdown (Optional) — Filter activities by designated task owner or assigned team member.
    - **Clear Filters** \* Button (System) — Resets active entity and owner filters to restore full calendar visibility.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Interactive Calendar View

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Calendar Layout & Controls" icon="calendar-days" color="#10B981">
    - **Navigation Controls**: Use the arrow buttons (`<`, `>`) or click **today** to jump across calendar periods.
    - **View Selector**: Toggle calendar display scale between **month**, **week**, and **day** layouts.
    - **Create Task Button**: Opens the task creation modal to log new activities and follow-ups.
    - **Calendar Grid**: Displays scheduled task blocks directly on their assigned due dates and time slots.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Task Preview Panel

Clicking a scheduled task block on the calendar opens a side preview panel with key details and quick actions:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Preview Fields & Action Controls" icon="sidebar" color="#8B5CF6">
    - **Task Headline & Title**: Full task name (e.g., _Follow up with Ravensburg Services on travel itinerary confirmation_).
    - **Type**: Classification of the activity entry (e.g., `Task`).
    - **Status**: Current execution state (e.g., `Scheduled`).
    - **Action Icons**:
      - **Edit (Pencil Icon)**: Opens the task editor modal to modify due dates, descriptions, or assignees.
      - **Delete (Trash Icon)**: Permanently removes the task entry from the calendar.
      - **Close (X Icon)**: Dismisses the preview side panel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

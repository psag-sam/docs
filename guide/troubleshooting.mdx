## A record has vanished

Almost always the record still exists and the person's role no longer reaches it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find who owns the record">
    Check the owner or assigned-to field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check whether that owner is inside the person's scope">
    Own covers only themselves. Team covers their team. Compare against the team structure, not the reporting line on the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rule out an unowned record">
    A record with no owner is invisible to everyone except roles set to Everyone. If ownership was never set, that alone explains it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A manager cannot see their team

Check that the reporting line appears in **Teams**. Teams are what drive access, and the manager field on a profile does not.

If a person was moved to a new manager after their account was created, the team structure may still show the old arrangement. This is the most common cause by some distance.

## Owner and assignment dropdowns are empty

The role needs at least **View** on Users to list people.

Grant View across Everyone. It lets the role see the staff directory, which is not the same as seeing the records those people own.

## Somebody sees nothing at all

Check they have a base role. A new user is created without one, and the symptom is empty lists everywhere rather than an error.

## A change has not taken effect

Permission changes reach people within a few seconds and do not need a fresh sign-in. If a change appears not to have applied, confirm that **Save changes** was pressed. The grid holds edits until then.

## Nobody can find Roles or Teams

Both screens are limited to system administrators and are reached from Settings rather than the sidebar.

## Good habits

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Start narrow" icon="compress">
    Widening access later is a short conversation. Explaining why somebody saw a record they should not have is a much longer one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prefer teams to one-off roles" icon="users">
    A role per person produces a grid nobody can audit six months from now.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Give View before Edit" icon="eye">
    Most access requests are really requests to look something up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep Administrator small" icon="shield">
    It bypasses record scoping entirely, so an administrator sees everything regardless of teams.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Go to **Settings**, then **Roles and permissions**. Every role is listed with a one line summary and a count of how many people hold it.

## Reading the grid

Open any role to see its permissions. They are grouped by area of the product, so the common case is a handful of decisions rather than ninety.

<Frame caption="The grid for Standard User. Sales is set to Full on Own records, so this person manages their own leads, accounts, contacts and deals and sees nobody else's.">
  <img src="/images/rbac/permission-grid.jpg" alt="The permission grid showing Can do and Whose records controls for each group" />
</Frame>

Three things on this screen are worth recognising.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Greyed-out controls" icon="ban">
    The setting does not apply. Some records, such as company settings, are not owned by any one person, so there is nothing to scope. Pearl shows these as an organisation-wide setting instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The word mixed" icon="shuffle">
    The objects inside that group do not all share one setting. Quoting and Billing shows mixed when invoices and tax configuration are set differently.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A custom chip" icon="tag">
    Counts how many objects inside the group have been set individually. It is your reminder that the group summary is hiding detail underneath.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Setting one object on its own

Click the sliders icon at the right of any group to expand it.

<Frame caption="Sales expanded. Leads, Accounts, Contacts and Deals can now each be set separately.">
  <img src="/images/rbac/grid-expanded.jpg" alt="The Sales group expanded to show individual rows for Leads, Accounts, Contacts and Deals" />
</Frame>

Set the group when a whole area behaves the same way, and expand only when one object genuinely needs to differ. A grid where everything has been set individually is hard to audit later.

<Warning>
Changing a group that contains custom settings replaces every individual setting inside it. Pearl asks you to confirm first. If you meant to keep those, cancel and set the objects one at a time instead.
</Warning>

## Saving

Changes are held until you press **Save changes**. Nothing takes effect while you are still adjusting the grid.

Once saved, the change reaches affected people within a few seconds. Nobody needs to sign out and back in.
